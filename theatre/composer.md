<p class="lead">Original music shaped around story, atmosphere, and the practical life of a production.</p>

Bendit develops music in conversation with directors, performers, and designers. The process can move from early musical ideas and workshops through to a complete score, always with attention to dramatic pacing and the relationship between music and text.
