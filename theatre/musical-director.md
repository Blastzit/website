<p class="lead">Musical leadership that connects the score, the performers, and the dramatic shape of a production.</p>

As a musical director, Bendit works with creative teams and performers from rehearsal through performance. His approach is collaborative and precise, with close attention to communication, pacing, and consistency across the production.

## Work includes

- Musical preparation and rehearsal
- Vocal and ensemble coaching
- Arrangements and score adaptation
- Performance leadership
