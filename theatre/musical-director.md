<p class="lead">Musical leadership that connects the score, the performers, and the dramatic shape of a production.</p>

As a musical director, Bendit works with creative teams and performers from rehearsal through performance. His experience spans keyboard-conducting, rehearsal piano, vocal coaching, orchestration and score preparation, with close attention to communication, pacing and consistency across a production.
