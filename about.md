    <p class="lead">Bendit Chan is a pianist, composer, and musical director working across classical performance and theatre.</p>

    <p>Bendit Chan is a Hong Kong-born pianist currently pursuing postgraduate studies at the Guildhall School of Music &amp; Drama in London under the tutelage of Professor Gareth Owen and Noriko Ogawa, where he is refining a wide-ranging repertoire and performance craft.</p>

    <p>Praised for mature musical insight and technical clarity, Chan has given solo recitals and appeared as a concerto soloist in major London venues. In March 2025, he performed Rachmaninoff’s Piano Concerto No. 3 at Cadogan Hall with the Imperial College Symphony Orchestra, conducted by Oliver Gooch. A year earlier, he performed Prokofiev’s Piano Concerto No. 3 at Imperial College London with the Imperial College Sinfonietta, conducted by Bill Carslake. Alongside his solo work, he is active as a chamber musician and regular ensemble collaborator.</p>

    <p>His performances combine stylistic sensitivity with expressive authority, from the intricate textures of twentieth-century repertoire to the broad architectural sweep of Romantic works. His thoughtful approach and engaging presence have been recognised by audiences and peers alike.</p>

    <p>Chan’s theatre work includes extensive experience with Imperial College Musical Theatre Society across composition, orchestration, musical direction, keyboard-conducting and rehearsal piano. With the society, he was composer, orchestrator and musical director for <em>DB Cooper: The Musical</em>, and musical director and keyboard-conductor for <em>The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee</em>. Earlier Imperial credits include Keys 1 for <em>Matilda</em> and Keys 1 and rehearsal pianist for <em>Big Fish</em>. He was also composer, orchestrator and musical director for <em>Sidekicked</em> at the UCL InterACT Festival.</p>

    <p>In recognition of his artistic promise, Chan received the Ede &amp; Ravenscroft Scholarship during his undergraduate studies at Imperial College London and a Guildhall School Scholarship upon admission for postgraduate study. An active participant in masterclasses and collaborative projects, he continues to build a performance profile grounded in individuality, stylistic integrity and thoughtful musicianship.</p>
