<p class="lead">Bendit Chan is a pianist, composer, and musical director working across classical performance and theatre.</p>

His work begins with close listening: to structure, colour, line, and the people in the room. At the piano, he approaches repertoire with clarity and a strong sense of narrative. As a composer and musical director, he works closely with performers and creative teams to build a coherent musical world.

Whether in recital or in rehearsal, the focus is the same—music that feels direct, considered, and alive.
