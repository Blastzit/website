<p class="lead">Before his work centred on music, Bendit studied Mathematics at Imperial College London.</p>

Mathematics remains an important part of how he thinks: through structure, abstraction, pattern, and the pleasure of following an idea to its conclusion. These notes span independent study during high school and subjects explored at university.

## Notes archive

The notes are personal working documents. Some are complete; others record an unfinished first pass through a subject and may contain errors or typographical mistakes.
